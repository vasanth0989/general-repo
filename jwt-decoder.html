JWT decoder

Paste an access token. Everything happens in this page; nothing is sent anywhere. Header and payload are only base64url-encoded, so they decode without any key. The signature cannot be verified here, since that needs the issuer's public key.

Expected on an FIS Sandbox token: exp - iat equal to the validity you requested (3,600 or 7,200 seconds), sub or a client id claim matching the consumer key, and an issuer on the FIS gateway domain. A token that decodes fine but is past exp produces the gateway's 401 "Invalid Credentials" (fault 900901).
