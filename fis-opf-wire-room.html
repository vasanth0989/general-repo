01 · The engine

What OPF actually is

FIS OPF is an enterprise payment-processing platform — the successor to FIS Quantum. Rather than building and certifying our own Fedwire, CHIPS, and SWIFT pipelines, we hand each approved wire to OPF and it does the industrial part.

Validate

Every wire is checked

Account validation, cut-off enforcement, duplicate detection, and OFAC / AML / regulatory screening — before anything moves.

Transform

Every format is spoken

Fixed-length files and XML become Fedwire messages, CHIPS formats, SWIFT MT/MX — with native ISO 20022 (pacs.008 / pacs.009) since 2022.

Route

Every rail is reachable

Domestic wires ride Fedwire or CHIPS; international ride SWIFT — with correspondent settlement and fallback routing if a network is down.

Carries: Fedwire · CHIPS · SWIFT MT & MX · FX · internal Naming note: BPM's “Quantum” step = OPF today Vendor: FIS (Fidelity Information Services)
02 · The journey

Follow one wire out of the bank

Eight stations between “send my money” and settled funds. Two humans approve every wire before it ever reaches OPF — the engine sits deliberately downstream of all judgment.

Station 01 · Initiate

The customer asks

A wire request arrives through web or mobile (Wires-App), or on paper, fax, and mail handled by Atlas agents. Validation services and the EDP fraud decision engine screen the request before a case even exists.

Wires-AppAtlasEDP Decisioning
Station 02 · Approve

Two humans say yes

An IBM BPM case walks the wire through validation, customer contact, a first approval, and a second approval. No wire moves on one person's judgment.

IBM BPM · Almanac Wires
Station 03 · Debit

The money is set aside

Finacle, the core banking system, debits the customer's account — systematically through the BPM workflow, manually only for CD and full-balance wires.

Finacle core
Station 04 · Collect

The batch gathers approved wires

The Wires Transfer Batch — a Spring Batch application scheduled by AutoSys — finds every case that has cleared second approval and writes them into a fixed-length request file for BT Payments. Every 30 minutes in the morning; every 15 after 1 PM.

Spring BatchAutoSys
Station 05 · Decorate

BT Payments dresses the file for OPF

The file rides DFTP — our in-house enterprise file transmission — to BT Payments (Pep+), the mainframe hub. Pep+ decorates the file into the exact format OPF requires, renaming it at every hop along the way.

Pep+ also cuts BCP files — pre-positioned backup copies of the payments. If OPF is ever down, we use those BCP files ourselves to process the critical wires manually through JPMorgan.

Station 06 · Process

OPF takes over

OPF validates the batch, enriches each payment — routing numbers, BICs, intermediary banks — and formats it for its rail. One bad case? The whole batch set gets a NACK and nothing moves — a deliberate safety property.

FIS OPF
Station 07 · Settle

BNY Mellon carries it home

We are not a direct Fedwire participant on this path — BNY Mellon, our correspondent bank, settles on our behalf: Fedwire for domestic dollars, SWIFT for international. FedLine Advantage exists only as a rare manual fallback.

BNY MellonFedwireSWIFT
Station 08 · Confirm

The status comes back on its own

OPF drops ACK files and per-wire XML; an AutoSys watcher spots them within five minutes, the response job parses every case, and BPM is updated over REST. The case confirms and closes — the customer gets a notification. No human in the loop.

04 · The answer

Every batch gets exactly one of two answers

ACK Batch accepted
  • ACK*.txt arrives with one XML per wire
  • AckStep reads the expected transaction count
  • WatchPSRFileStep waits until every XML lands
  • xmlProcessStep parses each case and posts its status to BPM via REST
  • Files archived — cases confirm & close
NACK Batch rejected
  • One failing case rejects the whole batch set — a deliberate safety property
  • The watcher archives the NACK; nothing else processes
  • Cases stay open in BPM for correction and ride the next scheduled batch
  • Ops investigates through BPM queues — nothing is silently lost

The OPF platform migration changed only this response flow — the request flow was untouched.

06 · Why it matters

What OPF buys us

ISO 20022, inherited

Fedwire migrated to ISO 20022 in July 2025. OPF has carried strong ISO support since 2022 — readiness we inherit rather than build.

Compliance as a platform feature

OFAC / AML screening, cut-offs, duplicate checks, and audit trails are OPF capabilities — not custom code we maintain and certify.

No home-grown pipelines

We never build our own Fedwire, CHIPS, or SWIFT connectivity. OPF is the orchestration layer between us and the networks.

Merger-friendly ground

FIS platforms are widely deployed and stable — a lower-risk anchor while Discover and Capital One payment stacks converge.

The one-liner for the room

One engine, every outbound wire — understand OPF and you understand how Discover moves money.
